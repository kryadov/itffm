# Infinite world: chunked generation — implementation plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-10-infinite-world-design.md`.

Branch: current branch. TDD throughout: failing test → minimal implementation
→ green → commit. One commit per task.

## Global constraints

- **Backward compatible underneath.** Every placement function gains an
  optional origin and keeps its existing tests passing unchanged when called
  the old way (whole plot, centred on world (0, 0)).
- A chunk is generated only from `(coord, globalSeed)` — never from how many
  objects some other chunk placed first.
- Chunk (0, 0) is the home plot and is never generated by the streamer.
- No real-place streaming in this pass: only `fellBackTo === 'demo'` streams.
- Fixed landmarks (hut, campfire, fisherman's hut, hive) are never per chunk.

## Task 1 — grid math, and an off-centre gridded terrain

- New `src/world/chunking.ts`: `CHUNK_SIZE` 400, `ChunkCoord`,
  `chunkCoordAt(x, z)`, `chunkOrigin(coord)`, `chunkSeed(coord,
  globalSeed)`, `chunksInRadius(center, r)`, `chunkKey(coord)`.
- `src/terrain/gridded.ts`: `gridProviderFromArray(..., origin?)` and
  `griddedProvider(source, halfSize, segments, origin?)` sample and answer
  in world coordinates around an origin.
- Tests (`test/world/chunking.test.ts`): origin in (0, 0); chunk (0, 0)
  centred on the origin; the boundary at exactly half a chunk; a 300 m home
  plot fits inside (0, 0); `chunkOrigin` round-trips with `chunkCoordAt`;
  `chunkSeed` deterministic, different for neighbours and for another world
  seed; `chunksInRadius` gives a (2r+1)² square centred on its chunk;
  `chunkKey` unique and stable; `CHUNK_SIZE` larger than the fog's 140 m and
  at least twice the largest home half-size. `test/terrain/gridded.test.ts` —
  offsets by origin without changing unoffset behaviour; resamples an
  off-centre chunk correctly.

Commit: `feat: chunk grid math, and an off-centre origin for gridded terrain`

## Task 4 — the chunk manager

- New `src/game/worldStream.ts`: `createWorldStream(scene, globalSeed, homeGround,
  homeRadius, species = loadSpecies()): WorldStream` with `update(playerX,
  playerZ)`, `pendingChunkCount()`, `mushroomObjects()`,
  `removeMushroomObject(obj)`, `obstacles()`, `updateLod(camera)`,
  `heightAt(x, z)`, `dispose()`.
  - Keeps `chunksInRadius(current, LOAD_RADIUS)` loaded; disposes anything
    beyond `UNLOAD_RADIUS` (hysteresis: a wobble on a boundary does not
    thrash).
  - Builds at most `BUILD_BUDGET_PER_UPDATE` (1) chunk per call, so a burst
    of newly needed chunks cannot stall one frame for seconds.
  - A chunk: procedural terrain at `chunkOrigin`, a ground tile, trees at
    `TREE_DENSITY`, `CHUNK_SITE_COUNT` sites, spawned placements built into
    world meshes.
  - `heightAt` defers to the home provider inside the home radius.
- Tests (`test/game/worldStream.test.ts`): never generates the home chunk
  though its neighbours preload; at most one chunk per `update()`; a 3×3
  block once the player leaves the home chunk and enough frames pass;
  deterministic per seed, different per seed; unloads far chunks; stable on
  a small wobble; no tree obstacle inside the home radius; home provider
  inside the radius; real terrain outside, continuous across the seam;
  placements only from loaded chunks; `removeMushroomObject` finds the owner
  or reports a miss; `updateLod` does not throw; `dispose` removes every
  chunk.

Commit: `feat: chunk streaming manager for the infinite demo wood`

## Task 5 — wire it in, demo wood only

- `src/game/loadForest.ts`: the demo wood (pressed or fallen back to) always
  builds its home plot at exactly `CHUNK_SIZE / 2`, whatever the size picker
  says; a named place is unchanged.
- `main.ts`: when `fellBackTo === 'demo'`, create the stream, call
  `update()` every frame, merge its obstacles and pickable objects with the
  home plot's, ground height from `stream.heightAt`, and drop the hard
  clamp to the plot square. A named place keeps the clamp.
- Spec: append "Scope actually shipped this pass" — why only the demo wood
  (the home-plot vs chunk-grid reconciliation; a real place's `halfSize` is
  also its OSM query radius).
- Live: headless walk to world (250, 0), past the old ±200 m edge —
  continuous ground and trees, no seam, no void.

Commit: `feat: infinite world for the demo wood`

## Task 6 — hares and squirrels beyond the home plot

- `src/util/openSpot.ts`: `findOpenSpot(..., worldCenter?)` — the clearance
  bound was always measured from world (0, 0); a chunk far out failed it at
  every candidate and silently returned an unchecked spot.
- `worldStream.ts`: per chunk, `CHUNK_HARE_COUNT`/`CHUNK_SQUIRREL_COUNT` (2
  each) from `placeCritterHomes`, seeded off the chunk; disposed with it;
  `updateCritters(dt, player)`.
- Tests: checks clearance around a `worldCenter` far from (0, 0); each loaded
  chunk has its own hares and squirrels, disposed when it unloads;
  `updateCritters` does not throw; `dispose` removes the wildlife too.

Commit: `feat: chunked hares and squirrels beyond the home plot`

## Task 7 — decorative scatter beyond the home plot

- `placeBoulders`, `placeLogs`, `placeStumps`, `placeLeaningTrees`,
  `placeBushes`, `placeFlora`, `placeGrass`: optional `chunkOrigin`.
- `worldStream.ts`: scatter each chunk at the home plot's densities —
  **visual only**, no ecology sites from logs and boulders. Measure first:
  feeding them into `buildSites` took one chunk from 200 to ~950 sites and
  its build from ~20 ms to ~78 ms, and the stream test suite from ~30 s to
  over two minutes. Revert that and ship the scatter alone.
- Tests: each placer "clusters around an optional chunkOrigin instead of
  world (0, 0)".
- Live: headless run ~900 m out — boulders and scatter in the scene graph,
  no console errors beyond headless Pointer Lock.

Commit: `feat: chunked decorative scatter beyond the home plot`
